00 / THE PREMISE

Everyone
belongs to someone.

In Night City, allegiances can be bought, forged, stolen, or broken. Corporations keep their secrets, fixers keep their commissions, and the dead keep nothing.

These nine individuals aren't a formal crew. Their lives intersect through favours, desire, old debts, and the occasional exceptionally well-paid contract. At the centre is Vaessa Vei: unaffiliated, dangerous, and loyal to the handful of people she calls her own.

03 / VIRTUAL PHOTOGRAPHY NOTES
SHORT SCENES / 3–9 FRAMES

SMALL
MOMENTS.

No single overarching storyline. Just lives that occasionally intersect in Night City.

These characters are designed for short virtual-photography stories and mini comics. Each scene might capture a private conversation, a magazine shoot, an infiltration, a tense corporate meeting, or a quiet night off.

Scene ideas: Vaessa returning from a contract to find Xinyi waiting up; Roxana bringing a ruined military vehicle to Viva’s garage; Ariella getting ready backstage while SYN-03 manages the chaos; Reina slipping Xinyi an encrypted shard over coffee; Kaya ignoring another warning from Alt; Samara and Vaessa arriving at an exclusive club together.
